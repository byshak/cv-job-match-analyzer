CV vs resume: what is the difference?

The words are often used interchangeably, but the meaning depends on where you are applying.

UK, Ireland and much of Europe

A CV is the standard application document: a one to two page summary of your experience, education and skills.

US and Canada

A resume is a short, targeted document, usually one page. A CV is longer and mostly used for academic, research and medical roles.

Quick comparison

TermTypical lengthUsed for
CV (UK/Europe)1-2 pagesMost jobs
Resume (US/Canada)1 pageMost jobs
Academic CVSeveral pagesResearch, teaching, medicine

Which should you send?

Follow the job advert and the country. If it says CV, send a CV; if it says resume, send a resume. For software and keyword matching, the content matters more than the label. Tailor it to the job either way.
