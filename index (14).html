        Questions

        Frequently asked questions

        Is my CV uploaded or stored?

        No. Everything runs in your browser. Your CV and the job description are not sent to a server and nothing is saved. PDF reading loads the pdf.js library, but the file itself stays on your device.

        Does a high score guarantee ATS approval?

        No. Results are guidance only. Every employer and ATS behaves differently, and this tool does not simulate any of them.

        How is the match score calculated?

        Keyword and skill coverage is 50%, key phrases 20%, job title alignment 15% and experience signals 15%. See the full method on the how scoring works page.

        Which files can I import?

        PDF, DOCX and TXT. Scanned PDFs without selectable text are not supported yet.
